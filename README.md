# SmartBudgetManagger
A project for managing Budget.
SmartBudget Manager
An Excel workbook to record your monthly income and expenses, sort expenses by category, see your balance and a chart, and get a warning when you spend more than your budget.
Version: 1.0 · File: `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx` · Currency: euro (€)
What it does
Records income and expenses (date, description, type, category, amount, notes)
Calculates Total Income, Total Expenses and Monthly Balance automatically
Shows expenses per category and a pie chart Expenses by Category
Displays WITHIN BUDGET (green) or OVER BUDGET (red) against your budget limit
What you need
Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365 (or compatible spreadsheet software)
No macros, no internet connection and no Excel formulas knowledge required
Quick start (2 minutes)
Open `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`. If you see a yellow Protected View bar, click Enable Editing.
Go to the Categories sheet and enter the Monthly Budget of each category.
Go to the Transactions sheet. In the first empty row (from row 4), fill in Date, Description, Type (list: Income / Expense), Category (list, expenses only) and the amount in Income or Expense.
Open the Dashboard sheet to read the totals, the warning and the chart.
Save with Ctrl + S.
The three worksheets
Worksheet	Purpose	Do you type here?
Transactions	Enter income and expenses	Yes
Categories	Category list and monthly budgets	Only the budget amounts
Dashboard	Totals, balance, warning, chart	No (calculated)
Good to know
Never type in the Month column; it is calculated.
Only rows 4 to 50 of Transactions are counted (47 transactions).
Use one copy of the file per month: totals add all rows.
The dashboard shows the five predefined categories (Food, Transport, Rent, Health, Education).
The warning compares total expenses with the overall budget, not with income.
